<h2>
🌸
About CliffTree
🌸
</h2>
</div>

**Cliff under a Tree**, or **CliffTree** for short, is a world generation datapack that freshens up the Overworld with brand new biomes and improvements to existing biomes, all while maintaining the spirit of vanilla!

From searing Shrublands to frigid Glacier Vallies, explore a plethora of biomes, including all-new sights such as the Oasis, the Tundra, or variations of familiar places, like the Snowy Old Growth Taiga!

<div align="center">
<h4>
Vanilla Biome Improvements
</h4>
</div>

Many biomes from vanilla have been improved! Alongside overall better distribution, CliffTree makes visual changes such as terracotta on the sides of Savanna Plateaus, fallen logs on various forest biomes, more stone on the sides of cliffs, and wide Beaches reminiscent of Minecraft beta.

<div align="center">
<h4>
Improved Atmosphere
</h4>
</div>

CliffTree is carefully designed with visual appeal in mind, so you'll find various tweaks to the environment such as fog distance, light tinting during sunrises and sunsets, variations in cloud colors, and much more!

<div align="center">
<h4>
⚠️ Sky Biomes (read disclaimer) ⚠️
</h4>
</div>

Above the clouds, you'll find dedicated biomes where rain does not fall and stars light up even during daytime.

**⚠️ Important Disclaimer**: these biomes can cause issues with terrain mods, so **they can be disabled** via config in the mod version, or by deleting or renaming the 
`overlay.sky_biomes`
 directory in the datapack version. With [Lithostitched](https://modrinth.com/mod/lithostitched), sky biomes will work with certain terrain mods like [Tectonic](https://modrinth.com/datapack/tectonic) without issue, but issues might arise if the settings are not default, especially ones related to terrain height, cloud height, and build height.

<div align="center">
<h4>
Improved Rivers and Oceans
</h4>
</div>

Nearly every biome in CliffTree has new colors for water, seen across the Overworld's rivers and oceans! Dive into even deeper oceans, and swim through new underwater rock and spire formations! River floors have also been altered, improving the distribution of sand, clay, and gravel underwater to provide a nice and cohesive gradient, rather than random disks.

<div align="center">
<h4>
New and Improved Cave Biomes
</h4>
</div>

Instead of surface biomes extending all the way down to the underground, CliffTree adds distinct cave biomes! You'll still find ordinary-looking caves, but you'll also find the ancient Dirt Caves, terracotta-filled Warm Caves, and deep down, the scorching magma of the Inferno!

<div align="center">
<h4>
New Tree Shapes and Foliage Rules
</h4>
</div>

Foliage like grasses and flowers can now be planted on sand snow blocks! Some trees have also received a makeover, such as Birch and small Jungle trees. Saplings also have custom growth rules, with only some saplings growing up on sand and snow, and certain blocks allowing the growth of rare tree types, like Jungle bushes and Swamp Oaks!

<h3>
Tectonic
</h3>

</div>

Tectonic is a world generation project that aims to transform how terrain is shaped. Terrain forms to continent-like landmasses, tailored to fit massive mountain ranges and new terrain types. Oceans feature smaller "islands", up to around a thousand blocks across!

Since **Tectonic 3.0**, the mod versions of **CliffTree** and **Tectonic** are natively compatible together! If you're using the datapack version, a [compatibility datapack](https://modrinth.com/datapack/clifftree-tectonic) is required.

Since **CliffTree 4.0**, the datapack version is now natively compatible with Tectonic out of the box, but the Sky Biomes overlay directory **needs to be disabled** to function correctly, unless [Lithostitched](https://modrinth.com/mod/lithostitched) is installed.
